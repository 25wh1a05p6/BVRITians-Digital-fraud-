📚 Learn About Digital Scams

Learn how scammers operate and how you can protect yourself.

🔐

OTP Scam

Scammers may pretend to be bank employees and ask for your OTP.

🔗

Phishing

Fake links and websites are used to steal passwords and banking information.

🏦

Fake KYC

Fraudsters may send fake KYC messages containing dangerous links.

💳

UPI Scam

Fake payment requests can trick users into entering their UPI PIN.

💼

Job Scam

Fake jobs may ask applicants to pay registration or processing fees.

📈

Investment Scam

Scammers may promise guaranteed high returns on investments.

🎁

Lottery Scam

Fake prize messages may ask you to pay a fee to receive the reward.

👤

Impersonation

Scammers pretend to be officials, friends or customer support agents.

🛡️ Stay Safe Online

  • Never share your OTP, PIN or password.
  • Do not click unknown or suspicious links.
  • Never send money to unknown people.
  • Verify information through official websites or apps.
  • Do not make financial decisions under pressure.
  • Be careful with unexpected prizes and investment offers.
