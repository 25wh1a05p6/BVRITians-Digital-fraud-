🔍 Why is this a Scam?

Our analysis found several warning signs in the message.

🚨

Potential Scam Detected

🔐

OTP Request

The message asks you to share an OTP. Never share your OTP with anyone. Banks and legitimate organizations do not need your OTP to receive a prize or verify an unexpected request.

⏰

Urgent Language

The message pressures you to act immediately. Scammers often create urgency so that users do not have enough time to verify the information.

🔗

Suspicious Link

The message contains a suspicious or unknown link. Such links may redirect you to fake websites designed to steal passwords, OTPs or banking information.

🎁

Unexpected Reward

The message claims that you have won a prize or received money without any known reason. Fake rewards are a common technique used by scammers.

🛡️ What Should You Do?

  • ❌ Do not click the suspicious link.
  • ❌ Do not share your OTP, PIN or password.
  • ❌ Do not send money to the sender.
  • ✅ Verify the information through the official website or app.
  • ✅ Block and report the suspicious sender.
